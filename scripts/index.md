## FAQ

### What is Starty, exactly?

Starty is the AI consumer app builder. You describe an app in plain words and Starty's agents build it, run it and grow it. Build covers agents, models, templates and the builder. Run covers compute, data, security, sign-in and payments. Grow covers messaging, referrals and paid growth. It is not a chatbot and not a code generator. It is the team that ships.

### How is Starty different from ChatGPT or other AI assistants?

Most AI tools hand you code or a plan and wait. Starty gives you a live app. It writes the code, sets up the database, sign-in and payments, puts the app on its own link and keeps it running. Then its Grow agents go and find users for it. You never copy-paste anything.

### What can Starty actually build?

Consumer apps: subscriptions, marketplaces, habit trackers, games, community apps, landing pages with waitlists, and the small tools people pay a few dollars a month for. If you can describe what a user sees and what they pay for, Starty can build it.

### Do I need to know how to code?

No. Starty is built for creators, non-technical founders and product designers. You describe, review and approve. If you do code, you can open the project and edit anything Starty wrote.

### Who owns the app?

You do. The code, the data, the domain and the users are yours. You can export the codebase at any time and take it anywhere.

### What does Starty connect to?

Over 3,200 tools, including Stripe, Supabase, Postgres, GitHub, Google Drive, Slack, Notion, HubSpot, Meta Ads, Google Ads and TikTok. If a tool is missing, Starty can build the integration.

### Is my data secure?

Yes. Each app runs in an isolated environment. Starty only touches the tools you explicitly connect. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we do not train on your data or your users' data.

### Where does my app run?

On Starty. Compute, database, storage, sign-in and payments are part of the platform, so there is nothing to host or configure. You can point your own domain at it and publish to the App Store or Google Play through the normal review process.

### How long does it take to build an app?

Minutes for a first version. Most people have a working app on a live link before they finish their coffee, then spend their time refining it rather than building it.

### Can Starty make mistakes?

Yes. Starty is capable, not infallible. It tests what it builds, checks its own work and asks for confirmation before high-stakes actions such as charging users, deleting data or deploying a breaking change. You stay in control.

### What if Starty can't do something?

It will tell you. If a feature needs a service it does not have, it will ask you to connect one. If something is genuinely outside its capabilities, it says so rather than guessing.

### How much control do the agents have?

A lot, inside limits you set. Every agent gets a hard spend ceiling and a human kill switch, and none of them can access your customers' personal data. Anything irreversible waits for your approval.

### What does "Grow" actually do?

Grow agents handle distribution: they write the launch posts and clips, set up referral loops, run and tune paid campaigns, and message your users. They report what moved and why, and they stop when the budget you set runs out.

### Can I publish to the App Store?

Yes. Starty packages the app, prepares the listing and submits it. Nothing ships through a review bypass. Your app goes through the normal App Store and Google Play cycles, and Starty tells you where it is in the queue.

### How do I get started?

Click "Get Started for Free" and describe your first app. Starty will ask a few questions, build a first version and hand you a live link. You will be iterating together within minutes.

### Can multiple people work on one app?

Yes. Invite co-founders, designers or a friend to the project. Everyone can talk to Starty about the same app, and Starty keeps one shared context so nobody has to repeat themselves.

### Can I export my code?

Yes, any time. Every project is a standard codebase you can download or push to your own GitHub repository. There is no lock-in and no proprietary runtime you have to keep.

### What happens to my data if I disconnect an integration?

Starty stops reading from that source immediately. Some derived context, such as notes or summaries, may remain in the project's working memory. If you want a full reset, the Clean Project option wipes everything Starty has stored while keeping your account.

### Is my data encrypted?

Yes. Data in transit is encrypted with TLS and data at rest with industry-standard encryption. Starty is covered under our SOC 2 Type 1 compliance, with Type 2 in progress, and we are pursuing ISO 27001.

### Is my app isolated from other people's apps?

Completely. Each app runs in its own environment with its own database and keys. Your code and your users' data are never shared with, or accessible to, other builders. The boundary is technical, not just policy.

### Can I delete my app and all my data?

Yes. You can delete a project, wipe its stored context and remove its integrations from your account settings at any time, or ask support to do it. You can also delete your account entirely.

### How does Starty use my prompts and app data?

Starty uses your prompts and project context only to build and run your app. It does not train external models on your data or your users' data, and nothing from your project is used for anyone else's app.

### What's on the roadmap for privacy and security?

Per-user API keys and scoped OAuth connections instead of shared tokens, role-based access for teams, configurable data retention with auto-purge, and automatic detection of sensitive content in prompts and data. These ship alongside stability as our top priority.
